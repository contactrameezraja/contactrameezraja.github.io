Rameez Raja · United Kingdom

Messy sources in. Trusted data out.

I'm a data and analytics engineer, but I started as an analyst, and that's still how I work. Before I touch a pipeline I want to know who's waiting on the number and what they'll do with it. That means sitting with the sales team in Spain, the finance team in the Netherlands, the product owner with the spreadsheet nobody dares to delete, and designing the model backwards from the decision.

The platforms I build on, Databricks, dbt, Snowflake, are just the means. The job is getting data people can trust into the room where it matters, and knowing when to push back on a number everyone already believes.

[03]How I can support you

Where I'm useful.

(01)

Warehouse to lakehouse migration

Redshift, Oracle or Snowflake to Databricks. Inventory, access, extraction, bronze landing, logic rebuild, side by side validation, cutover. Done it end to end.

(02)

dbt transformation layer

Staging, intermediate and marts under version control, with tests that fail in dev before production ever sees bad data. Turns spreadsheets and scripts into governed sources.

(03)

AI products on governed data

Genie spaces, instruction sets and UAT, delivered where people already work. The model underneath has to be clean first, and that's the part I own.

(04)

Governance and environments

Unity Catalog design, row level visibility, dev to UAT to prod promotion through Git and CI, formal sign off before anything reaches production.

(05)

Data storytelling and the business case

Translating data quality into hours and money leadership can act on. The cost of bad data model at Shell turned a field completion problem into a funded fix.
