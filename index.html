Messy sources in. Trusted data out.

I started out as an analyst. What hooked me was the idea that behind every number is a story, and that the job is getting that story to the people who need it.

Then the questions changed. How does a number actually reach Power BI or Tableau? What happens when it breaks, and why did nobody notice? Chasing those answers is what pulled me into engineering.

Today I work on both sides. The people I build for don't talk in pipelines or fields. They talk in numbers, and they want answers that are clear and short. So I build with them in the room, and I make sure what lands in front of them is something they can trust and act on.

[01]The Pipeline

What the journey looks like.

Most of my career has been migrations, moving data out of legacy systems and into something newer. They take time and a lot of planning, but they all end in the same place, one source everyone goes to. The pipeline is only half of it though. I work end to end, getting the data ready and then presenting it, because how a number is shown matters as much as how it got there. Here are three from my recent work.
