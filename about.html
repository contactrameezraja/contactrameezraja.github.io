Biography

Rameez Raja is a data and analytics engineer based in the United Kingdom, with twelve years spent turning disconnected source systems into data that businesses can act on. He started as an analyst, chasing numbers through spreadsheets and systems that did not agree with each other, and that is still how he approaches the work. Before touching a pipeline, he wants to know who is waiting on the number and what they will do with it.

His career runs through financial services, life sciences and energy, and across continents, with time working in Northern Ireland and New York and study exchanges in China. At DLL Group, part of Rabobank, he built the foundations in SQL, warehousing and reporting. At Citi he designed a medallion architecture on Snowflake for People Analytics and replaced forty hours a week of manual regulatory reporting with a single scheduled Python run, work that was adopted across Global Markets and recognised with the Innovation Award. At Abcam, part of Danaher, he migrated the Oracle and Redshift warehouse onto Databricks and rebuilt its transformation layer as tested dbt models. Most recently at Shell Energy he delivered Customer 360 across five European markets under Unity Catalog and launched SEBI, the first Genie deployment into Microsoft Teams across Shell.

He is the founder of Aleatoric Lab, an independent research consultancy focused on probabilistic machine learning and uncertainty quantification, with conference publications at ICML and NeurIPS. He is a member of the British Academy Early Career Researcher Network, and a contributor to the MLCommons AI Risk and Reliability working group. He holds an MSc in Predictive Modelling and Scientific Computing from the University of Warwick and a BSc in Mathematics from The Open University.
